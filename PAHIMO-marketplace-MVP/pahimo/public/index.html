Starter Partner, payout & fee policy · draft

A proposed inclusion policy for review. No verification, fee collection, or payouts are active in this demo.

  • Starter Partner: welcome people without government ID, label the profile “Starter / not ID-verified,” allow one listed service, and use a lower per-booking limit (amount to be decided). Some higher-risk services may need extra checks.
  • Online-paid bookings: record the job and itemized price; pay out only through an approved provider to a payout method confirmed for that Partner. No bank or GCash/Maya details in ordinary chat. If they need help, offer a secure support setup; do not promise payout until a valid route is available.
  • Fee-free cases proposed: zero PAHIMO fee on cash jobs, and for Pabili/Pabakal with goods subtotal under ₱100 within the same barangay. This waives only PAHIMO’s platform fee—not the Partner’s agreed service/labor or transport amount.
  • Other paid jobs: set a modest disclosed fee based on the Partner’s service charge only, excluding item purchases and reimbursed expenses; add a cap so the platform fee never exceeds that service charge. The rate and cap are still undecided.
  • Wallet/bank transfer costs: show provider charges separately before acceptance; never hide them inside the PAHIMO fee. The Partner should agree to any pass-through cost before the booking.

If a payout method belongs to someone else, do not accept it through a casual chat message. Any assisted payout would need an approved process, consent, and ownership checks.
